About the Club

A place for readers, learners, and confident voices.

The Read, Learn and Shine Club brings together students of the Department of Library and Information Science, NEHU Shillong, to build a strong reading culture and create opportunities for thoughtful expression. The club supports book discussions, information literacy, public speaking, creative writing, peer learning, and community outreach.

R

Read

Build regular reading habits through books, articles, journals, and library resources.

L

Learn

Share ideas, improve information skills, and learn from classmates and faculty mentors.

S

Shine

Grow confidence through presentations, discussions, writing, and public engagement.

What We Do

Club Activities

Book Talks

Members present books, authors, themes, and reading reflections in friendly sessions.

Reading Circles

Small group reading sessions for fiction, non-fiction, professional literature, and current topics.

Information Literacy

Workshops on library use, reference tools, research sources, citation, and digital resources.

Creative Expression

Poetry, essays, posters, reviews, storytelling, and student-led knowledge displays.

Outreach

Reading promotion activities for schools, communities, and library users around Shillong.

Seminars and Events

Invited talks, observance days, competitions, exhibitions, and collaborative departmental events.

Membership

Open to interested students of DLIS, NEHU Shillong.

Students who enjoy reading, discussion, writing, organizing events, or helping others discover library resources can participate. Faculty coordinators may guide programs, meetings, and outreach activities.
